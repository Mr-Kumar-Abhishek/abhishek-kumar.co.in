Privacy Policy

Anonymous Menstrual Tracker

Our Philosophy: Zero Data

We built the Anonymous Menstrual Tracker with one fundamental principle: your body is your business, and your data belongs to you.

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any of your health data, personal information, or usage habits.

Local Storage Only

Everything you enter into the app—your cycle dates, symptoms, notes, and predictions—is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.

We do not have servers, databases, or cloud syncing mechanisms. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your records are permanently deleted. We have zero access to your information at any time.

Google Play Store Requirements

As the app is distributed via the Google Play Store, Google may automatically collect minimal, anonymized analytical data necessary for the app store ecosystem to function. This includes:

  • Basic installation metrics (e.g., number of downloads)
  • Crash logs and ANR (App Not Responding) reports
  • Device models and OS versions for compatibility

This information is standard across all Google Play apps, is completely anonymous, and is processed entirely by Google. We only see aggregate numbers to ensure the app is functioning properly. None of this data is linked to your identity or your health information.

No Third-Party Trackers

Unlike many other health apps, we do not integrate any third-party SDKs for advertising, user tracking, or behavioral analytics (e.g., no Firebase, no Facebook Pixel, no Mixpanel).

Updates to this Policy

Because our app does not collect data, this privacy policy is unlikely to change. However, if any legal or platform requirements necessitate an update, we will revise this page. The date of the latest revision will always be displayed below.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or how your app functions securely offline, please reach out to the developer directly.
